Counterpart: “Escape Velocity”

I felt you long
before you arrived—
a flicker
at the edge of my field.

You burned bright,
cutting your way inward
too fast
to slow.

I tried to widen my orbit,
give you room to settle,
but you wanted fusion
before equilibrium.

I’ve learned
what happens
when bodies collide
without time.

So when the spin went wild,
when your pull distorted mine,
I let gravity do
what it must—

release.

Not because you were wrong,
not because I didn’t feel it,
but because
I know my limits.

You mistook survival
for rejection.

I watched you drift away,
knowing someday
you’d meet a mass
that could hold you—
and I would remain
what I am:
a stable orbit,
not meant
for everyone
who passes close.

title: "Our Angel"
---

The room lit up when she walked in.<br> 
With childlike innocence and giggles<br>
She moved like a butterfly,<br> 
Brightening up everything & everyone around her.<br> 
She was such a joy to just watch.<br> 

She found joy in giving so much for nothing in return,<br>
While you confused stuff with stuff that really mattered.<br>
But now she has left, leaving you orphaned.<br>

You cry your heart out, refusing to say goodbye.<br>
Longing for a chance... for one last, long embrace.<br>
Just one more glimpse to say Thank You..,<br> 
Would’ve, Could’ve, Should’ve - regret cuts like a knife.<br>

She was there when you first saw the world,<br>
Now she is only there when you close your eyes,<br>
But sadly she is only visible so... so invisible,<br>
And fades away as you open your eyes.<br>

In loving memory of my beautiful beautiful Chitta who left on 11/21/2022.<br>
Will miss you till my last breath Chitta.

title: "To Chitta"
---

Another tearing autumn has arrived,<br>
Trees have shed their leaves mourning you..<br>
Many have come and gone since you left<br>
It's our first trip 'round the sun without you<br>
But we are frozen in last November.<br>

Every waking moment we long so bad..<br>
To hear that crackling laugh again..,<br>
To taste that lip-smacking feast once more,<br>
To see that alley lit up in spirits again.<br>

They say heaven is for the ones cherished here<br>
You will be there another century for sure.<br>
Cause you've touched generations to cherish you<br>
To carry your light in their hearts for lifetimes.<br>

Now I’m envy of you.., you’ll stay young for ever.<br> 
You cheated death.. he is the fool...<br> 
Taking you soon, he made you immortal.<br>

Long live our hero, our angel..,<br> 
Our effervescent butterfly..<br>
Let this be our incomplete parting song<br>
So long.., till we meet again. <br> 

love.
